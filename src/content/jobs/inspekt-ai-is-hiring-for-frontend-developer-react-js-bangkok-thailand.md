### About Inspekt AI

Inspekt AI is revolutionizing the property inspection industry with our cutting-edge SaaS platform powered by advanced AI technology. Our solution enables building owners, property managers, and contractors to conduct efficient, precise inspections that improve safety, save time, and reduce costs. We are seeking a talented Frontend Developer (React.js) to help build engaging, user-friendly interfaces that bring our platform to life for users across the globe.

### Job Description

Are you passionate about creating dynamic, user-friendly software solutions for a global user base? We are looking for a skilled Frontend Developer with expertise in React.js to join our team. You will play a pivotal role in shaping our SaaS platform, collaborating with designers and developers to build innovative interfaces that leverage the power of our AI technology. Your contributions will directly impact the way our users interact with and experience building inspections.

### Key Responsibilities

- Develop and maintain new user-facing features for our web platform.
- Build reusable, efficient, and modular React components to enhance scalability.
- Collaborate with UX/UI designers to translate mockups into responsive, visually appealing user interfaces.
- Write clean, maintainable code that adheres to best practices and coding standards.
- Ensure the technical feasibility of UI/UX designs across multiple devices and platforms.
- Debug and optimize front-end performance to deliver fast-loading pages and seamless user experiences.
- Stay up to date with the latest developments and advancements in React.js and related technologies.

### Qualifications

- Proven experience as a Frontend Developer, with a strong portfolio of projects built with React 16.8 or above.
- Proficiency in TypeScript and JavaScript.
- Thorough understanding of React.js core principles (e.g., hooks, lifecycle methods, context API).
- Familiarity with modern UI/CSS frameworks such as TailwindCSS or Material UI.
- Strong problem-solving skills and a detail-oriented mindset.
- Ability to understand business requirements and translate them into technical implementations.
- Excellent communication skills in English, with a team-oriented mindset and a proactive work ethic.

### Good to Have

- Experience in game development with knowledge of 3D mechanisms, linear algebra, and physics principles.
- Familiarity with cloud services such as AWS, Azure, or GCP.
- Experience integrating analytics tools (e.g., Google Analytics, Mixpanel) into frontend code.
- Proficiency in writing frontend tests using frameworks such as Jest, Cypress, or React Testing Library.
- Experience in mobile application development using React Native.
