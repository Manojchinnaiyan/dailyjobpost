### 

Software Development Engineer 1 – Frontend (Web)

Location: Bengaluru

At Licious, we’re building a technology-first food brand that delivers fresh meat and seafood to millions of customers. Our engineering team works on highly scalable systems that power a seamless and delightful customer experience.

We are looking for a Software Development Engineer 1 – Frontend (Web) who is passionate about building fast, scalable, and user-friendly web applications.

### What You’ll Do

- Build and maintain high-quality web applications using modern frontend frameworks.
- Develop responsive and user-friendly interfaces that deliver a seamless customer experience.
- Work closely with product managers, designers, and backend engineers to ship new features.
- Optimize web applications for performance, scalability, and reliability.
- Integrate frontend applications with RESTful APIs and backend services.
- Debug, troubleshoot, and improve existing web features and workflows.
- Ensure best practices in code quality, testing, and performance optimization.

### What We’re Looking For

- 1–3 years of experience in frontend development.
- Strong proficiency in React.js or Next.js.
- Solid understanding of HTML, CSS, JavaScript, or TypeScript.
- Experience building responsive and performant web applications.
- Familiarity with RESTful APIs and state management libraries
- Good understanding of web fundamentals, browser rendering, and debugging tools.

### Nice to Have

- Experience with micro-frontends architecture.
- Familiarity with server-side rendering (SSR).
- Knowledge of SEO optimization for web applications.
- Experience optimizing Web Vitals and frontend performance metrics.

### Why Join Licious

- Work on high-scale consumer products used by millions.
- Collaborate with a strong engineering and product team.
- Opportunity to build impactful technology in a fast-growing company
