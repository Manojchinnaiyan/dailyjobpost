JOB TITLE: Data Engineering Intern

REPORTS TO: Database Manager

DEPARTMENT: Product & Technology

LOCATION: Remote

DURATION: 8 Weeks starting June 15th

PAY RATE: $18.00 Per Hour

### JOB OVERVIEW

We are looking for a passionate and detail-oriented Data Engineering Intern to join our team! As a Data Engineering Intern, you’ll work closely with experienced data engineers and gain hands-on experience building and maintaining data pipelines, optimizing databases, and contributing to data-driven projects. This is an excellent opportunity to kickstart your career in data engineering while working on meaningful projects in a collaborative environment. The salary for this role is $18.00 per hour.

### COMPANY BACKGROUND

Prometric (www.prometric.com) is a trusted test development and delivery provider to more than 300 organizations worldwide. Prometric serves as an industry gatekeeper, ensuring that people legitimately earn the credentials they seek to achieve, and thereby guaranteeing a fair testing experience for all who come through our doors.

### RESPONSIBILITIES

Key Responsibilities:

- Assist in designing, developing, and maintaining scalable ETL/ELT pipelines to process data from various sources.
- Support the creation and optimization of data storage solutions, including data lakes and warehouses.
- Work with large and complex datasets to ensure accuracy, consistency, and security.
- Collaborate with data scientists, analysts, and engineers to understand data needs and requirements.
- Monitor and troubleshoot data pipelines to ensure efficient and error-free data processing.
- Work with Database engineers to understand the infrastructure that backends data platforms

### Qualifications:

- Currently pursuing or recently completed a degree in Computer Science, Data Engineering, or a related field.
- Familiarity with programming languages like Python, SQL, or Java.
- Basic knowledge of relational databases (e.g., SQL Server, Oracle, MySQL) and data modeling concepts.
- Understanding of big data tools or cloud platforms (e.g. Azure, AWS, Apache Spark) is a plus.
- Strong problem-solving skills and attention to detail.
- Eagerness to learn and collaborate in a team-oriented environment.
