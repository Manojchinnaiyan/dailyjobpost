Are you passionate about turning data into insight? Want to work in a fast-paced environment at the heart of Premier League football?

West Ham United F.C. is looking for a Data Analyst to support key commercial and marketing decisions across the Club. This is an exciting opportunity to play a central role in shaping fan engagement, driving revenue growth, and supporting long-term strategic planning through data.

Working alongside the CRM & Data Lead, you’ll support departments including Ticketing, Hospitality, Partnerships and Retail by transforming data into clear, actionable insight.

We are committed to building an inclusive workplace and welcome applications from individuals with different backgrounds, experiences and perspectives. We believe diverse teams help drive better ideas, stronger collaboration and greater success on and off the pitch.

### ⚽ WHAT YOU’LL BE DOING

- 📊 Identifying opportunities to grow matchday and non-matchday revenue
- 📈 Creating predictive models to improve marketing performance and fan engagement
- 📋 Building dashboards, reports and automated insights for senior leadership
- 🎟️ Supporting fan experience initiatives with data-led recommendations
- 🔐 Maintaining CRM database quality and GDPR compliance
- 🤝 Collaborating with teams across marketing, commercial, digital and content
- 📣 Creating audience segments and profiling to support targeted campaigns

### 🧠 WHAT WE’RE LOOKING FOR

- ✔ Degree (or equivalent) in a STEM or marketing-related discipline
- ✔ Minimum 2 years’ experience in a data or analytics role
- ✔ Strong SQL plus Python or R experience
- ✔ Experience using Tableau or Power BI
- ✔ Advanced Excel and PowerPoint skills
- ✔ Experience working with large datasets
- ✔ Strong attention to detail and organisational skills
- ✔ Ability to manage multiple projects in a fast-paced environment
- ✔ Interest in football or sport
