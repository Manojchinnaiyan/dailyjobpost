### About Shopflo

At Shopflo, we're trying to change the way consumers experience brands and businesses. Our first product was a cart and checkout platform for e-commerce, that allowed marketers to personalise discounts, rewards, and payments. We are currently also working on a new product that takes it a notch higher by unlocking enterprise-grade personalization for all consumer tech businesses.

### Team & Company

Shopflo was founded by three co-founders:
Ankit Bansal (ex-IIT Kharagpur, Oracle, Gupshup)
Ishan Rakshit (ex-IIT Bombay, Parthenon, Elevation Capital)
Priy Ranjan (ex-IIT Madras, McKinsey, Elevation Capital)

We’re a fast-growing team of ~50 people, based in HSR Layout, Bengaluru. We raised a $3.8M seed round from Tiger Global and TQ Ventures.

### What you will do

- Design, build, and maintain automated test suites for Shopflo’s core product features
- Execute manual testing for new features, edge cases, and regression scenarios
- Develop reliable end-to-end, integration, and regression tests for web applications and APIs
- Work closely with engineers to ensure features are built with strong test coverage
- Integrate automated tests into CI/CD pipelines to improve release confidence
- Identify, debug, and track product issues across the stack
- Create and maintain test cases, test plans, and documentation
- Ensure product quality across checkout, payments, and merchant-facing experiences
- Monitor releases and proactively identify potential regressions or performance issues

### You should apply if you have

- 1 to 3 years of experience in QA engineering, manual testing, or SDET roles
- Experience with both manual testing and test automation
- Strong programming skills in languages such as JavaScript, Python, or Java
- Experience with automation frameworks such as Playwright, Cypress, or Selenium
- Familiarity with testing APIs, integration flows, and frontend applications
- Understanding of CI/CD systems and automated testing pipelines
- Strong debugging and problem-solving skills
- Ability to collaborate closely with engineering teams in a fast-paced environment
