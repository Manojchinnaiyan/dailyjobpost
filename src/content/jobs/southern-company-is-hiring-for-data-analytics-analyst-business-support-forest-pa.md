### JOB SUMMARY

The Power Delivery Business Support Team plays a critical role in administrative, financial and technology functions of the physical construction of Georgia Power transmission and distribution assets including, but not limited to, Transmission Construction, GRID, Distribution Construction, Network Underground, Make Ready, Asset Management, and Centralized Engineering.

The Data Analytics Analyst will provide analytical support to the Power Delivery Construction leadership teams by reviewing and tracking specific project costs as well as researching spending patterns throughout the Construction organization. Field time with construction crews may be necessary to understand work practices, processes, and job requirements in order to assist with back-office operations. This role partners closely with internal technology and digital teams to leverage data, automation, and innovative solutions that enhance Power Delivery Business Support processes, improve system performance, and drive operational efficiency. The successful candidate will need to establish effective relationships and communications with field personnel, multiple levels of management, and Supply Chain.

The position will be filled at an exempt level with the actual level depending on the knowledge, skills and abilities of the successful candidate.

The work location for this position is remote but will require some travel.

### MAJOR JOB RESPONSIBILITIES

- Vendor escalations with issues related to CATEE/CAMP/ES Maximo
- Vendor onboarding to CATEE/CAMP
- CATEE troubleshooting and training
- CAMP troubleshooting and training
- Gather cost data and analyze capital spending
- Develop metrics & management reporting to provide Power Delivery stakeholders with necessary information to make well-informed decisions
- Assess and implement process improvements and best practices through automation and other technical solutions to drive efficiency
- Design and deliver Power BI reporting solutions that enhance business unit decision‑making
- Project management skills and an ability to achieve results through teamwork in the midst of competing priorities and potentially conflicting interests
- Coordinate special projects by gathering information and providing analysis
- Establish and maintain effective relationships and communications with internal and external customers
- Other projects & ad hoc reporting as needed
- Assist with storm restoration duties as needed
