### See Your Work in a New Light

At LUX Infusion, we believe infusion care deserves more light — more warmth, more clarity, and more connection. That commitment extends beyond clinical care to the financial processes that ensure patients can begin and continue therapy without unnecessary barriers.

We’re looking for a Billing Specialist who wants their work to mean something — someone who brings accuracy, accountability, and purpose to the reimbursement process that supports patient access to care.

### Why This Role Matters

Billing accuracy and timeliness are essential to keeping patients on therapy and ensuring care teams can focus on what matters most — the patient. The Billing Specialist plays a critical role in verifying reimbursement data, submitting clean claims, and resolving payments appropriately.

In this role, you’ll contribute to work that:

- Helps patients maintain uninterrupted access to infusion therapy
- Supports clinicians and operations teams through reliable revenue‑cycle execution
- Ensures compliance with payer contracts and reimbursement standards
- Strengthens a patient‑first, clinician‑led organization focused on outcomes

Behind every successful infusion is a billing process that works — and this role helps make that happen.

### What You’ll Do

In this role, you will:

- Prepare and submit insurance claims for home infusion services, including medications, nursing, and supplies
- Process electronic pharmacy claims to Medicare Part D, Medicaid, and commercial insurance plans
- Verify that all prescription reimbursement data is complete, accurate, and entered timely prior to weekly and month‑end close
- Confirm documentation aligns with billed services, codes, and expected reimbursement
- Review payments received and apply them accurately to patient accounts through final resolution
- Maintain a strong understanding of payer contracts, coverage criteria, and reimbursement guidelines
- Address billing inquiries and resolve discrepancies with professionalism and clarity
- Stay current on billing regulations, coding guidelines, and payer requirements
- Work with clearinghouses and billing systems to support clean claims and efficient workflows
- Assist with data entry, administrative, and clerical billing tasks as needed
