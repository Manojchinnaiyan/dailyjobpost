We are a growing U.S.-based healthcare services organization currently undergoing a system migration from a legacy platform to a new internal system.

To support this initiative, the company is seeking a dedicated Data Entry Specialist to assist with high-volume, accuracy-critical data transfer over a defined project timeline.

The Data Entry Specialist will support a 6-month data migration project, responsible for transferring large volumes of information from a legacy system into a new platform.

This role requires strong focus, consistency, and exceptional attention to detail. It is ideal for someone who performs well in structured, process-driven work environments.

### Key Responsibilities

- Perform high-volume data entry from a legacy system into a new platform
- Review and validate data for accuracy and completeness
- Follow documented migration procedures and workflows
- Identify missing or inconsistent information and flag for review
- Maintain strict quality control standards
- Track progress against daily and weekly targets
- Collaborate with internal stakeholders during the migration process
- Maintain confidentiality and data security at all times

### Required Qualifications

- 2+ years of experience in data entry, administrative support, or operations
- Exceptional attention to detail and data accuracy
- Strong organizational and time-management skills
- Comfort working with spreadsheets and internal systems
- Ability to perform repetitive tasks with consistency
- Strong written English communication skills
- Ability to work independently in a remote environment

### Preferred Qualifications

- Experience supporting data migrations or system transitions
- Familiarity with healthcare, regulated, or sensitive data environments
- Experience working in structured, process-heavy roles
