### About JHC Consulting

JHC Consulting is a private advisory practice that provides counsel to chief executives, chairs, and board committees on consequential decisions. The firm specializes in written analysis designed to remain on file and be referred to long after a meeting concludes. Rather than delivering polished presentations or facilitating endless consensus sessions, the firm produces memoranda that are authored, signed, and owned by a single individual. This approach rests on the belief that a well reasoned, seven page document is more valuable than a forty page slide deck that only surrounds a question.

We work with clients navigating complex governance transitions, strategic shifts, and structural reorganizations where the stakes of a decision are high. All engagements are grounded in the discipline of absolute discretion and analytical honesty. The work is precise and demanding, favoring clarity over volume and evidence over assumption. By focusing on the written record, the firm ensures that its counsel provides a stable and intellectually honest foundation for the future of the organizations it serves.

### The Role

The Project Coordinator Intern supports the consultants in the production of high stakes advisory documents. This position exists to ensure that every memorandum is supported by accurate research and organized with total precision. You will spend your time facilitating the transition of a project from its initial inquiry to a completed, signed document. This is not a clerical role in the traditional sense. It requires someone who can follow the thread of a complex argument and identify where the reasoning requires more evidence or where the historical context needs to be fleshed out.

### What You Will Do

- Conduct detailed research on corporate governance structures and historical business precedents.
- Fact check draft memoranda to ensure every statement of fact is supported by primary sources.
- Track project timelines to ensure that document production aligns with board meeting schedules.
- Organize client files and maintain the firm digital archive for long term reference.
- Draft initial sections of internal reports regarding market conditions or regulatory changes.
- Edit documents for clarity and adherence to the firm specific writing style.

### What We Are Looking For

- Exceptional written communication skills with a focus on brevity and logic.
- A disciplined approach to work that allows for long periods of solitary focus.
- High levels of discretion and the ability to handle confidential and sensitive information.
- Total attention to detail in spelling, grammar, and factual accuracy.
- The ability to organize complex information into a clear and readable structure.
- A preference for working with documents over participating in video meetings.

### Helpful Background

While we do not require specific professional experience for this internship, candidates with an academic background in law, history, political science, or philosophy often find our work matches their training well. These disciplines emphasize the importance of primary sources and logical argumentation, which are the tools we use every day. An interest in how large organizations make decisions and a desire to understand the mechanics of corporate governance are also beneficial. We value candidates who have spent time developing their own writing and can demonstrate a commitment to precision.

### Working at JHC Consulting

Working at JHC Consulting means joining an environment that prioritizes deep work and quiet thought. We are a fully remote firm, which allows our team members to structure their days around their most productive hours. We value intellectual honesty and expect everyone, including interns, to contribute to the rigor of our analysis. You will receive direct feedback on your writing and research, with a focus on developing a precise and professional voice. There is no expectation of performative busyness, only an expectation of quality.

Our firm is built on the longevity of our advice. We do not chase trends or use management buzzwords. Instead, we focus on producing work that will be useful years after it is written. This role is a fit for someone who values the discipline of the written record and wants to see how rigorous thinking is applied to the most difficult problems in leadership. We provide a space where your primary contribution is your ability to think and write with clarity.
