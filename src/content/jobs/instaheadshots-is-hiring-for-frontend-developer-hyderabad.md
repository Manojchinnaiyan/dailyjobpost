### What You'll Build & Learn

- Architect and ship production systems that handle AI image generation at scale - managing async job queues, designing APIs for thousands of concurrent requests, and building frontends that feel instant even when processing happens in the background.
- Work with generative AI models in production, not toy projects or demos. Make real architectural decisions: how to structure data models for rapid iteration, when to denormalize, how to handle eventual consistency, and where to cache.
- Identify what needs to be built, design the system, write the code, deploy it, and own it in production. Instrument everything, read the metrics, and iterate based on what you learn.
- Your decisions directly impact performance, reliability, and revenue - most engineers never get to build something from scratch that scales to thousands of paying users.

### Who Should Apply

- You've built and shipped non-trivial full-stack applications. You understand architectural trade-offs and can articulate why you chose one approach over another.
- You're comfortable reading unfamiliar codebases, debugging production issues, and working across the entire stack without hand-holding. You know when to optimize and when to ship. Strong opinions, loosely held.
- No prior AI experience required, but you should be excited to learn it deeply - not just call an API, but understand what's happening under the hood and how to optimize it.
- If you need detailed requirements, extensive onboarding, or a well-defined scope to be productive, this isn't the right role.

- 0–4 years of full-stack experience, primarily frontend
- Strong experience with React or Next.js
- Solid backend experience in any language
