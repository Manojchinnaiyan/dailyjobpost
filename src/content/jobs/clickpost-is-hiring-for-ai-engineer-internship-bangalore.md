### **About the Role:**

We’re looking for an AI Engineer Intern who can help bring AI ideas, internal automations, and local prototypes into production systems used across logistics and e-commerce operations at scale.

At ClickPost, we process millions of shipment events and power logistics operations for leading e-commerce brands. We’re now building AI-first systems across recommendations, support, operations, analytics, onboarding, and delivery intelligence.

If you love building with LLMs, shipping fast, and solving messy real-world problems with AI, this role is for you.

### What you'll work on:

You’ll work closely with Product, Engineering, and Data teams on AI systems across logistics workflows.

Projects may include:

- Converting internal AI experiments and scripts into production-ready services
- Building AI copilots for operations, support, onboarding, and analytics
- Creating internal search systems across logs, dashboards, configs, and documentation
- Building AI agents for debugging, RCA generation, and operational workflows
- LLM-powered recommendation and automation systems
- Prompt engineering, evaluation pipelines, and feedback loops
- RAG systems on logistics and operational data
- AI-powered analytics and insights generation
- Workflow automations for customer success and operations teams
- Integrating AI systems into existing ClickPost products and dashboards

### Tech Stack/ Area:

You will work on:

- Python
- FastAPI / Flask
- OpenAI / Claude / Gemini APIs
- LangChain / LlamaIndex
- Vector databases & embeddings
- SQL & analytics systems
- Redis / queues / async workflows
- Docker & cloud deployments
- Monitoring and observability systems

### What we are looking for:

### Must Have:

- Strong Python fundamentals
- Strong problem-solving ability
- 1-2 AI projects you've actually shipped: share GitHub, a demo link, or a writeup in your application
- Comfort with APIs and backend systems
- Ability to independently ship projects (take an idea from "works on my laptop" to "running in prod")

### Good To Have:

- Hands-on experience with LLMs and AI agents
- RAG pipelines / embeddings knowledge
- Familiarity with cloud deployment
- Prior hackathon or open-source work

### What Matters More Than Your Resume:

- You build things and experiment fast
- You can turn ideas into working systems
- You’re curious about applied AI, not just theory
- You care about impact and usability
