### Summary/Objective:

The Associate Application Developer works under the guidance of the Application Development team to provide top quality solutions that meet application solution needs of Elevations Credit Union business line departments. This is an early career role responsible for translating software requirements into well-designed solutions; working with 'off the shelf' software, modifying it and integrating it into the existing architecture; and maintaining programs for business applications.

### Essential Functions:

- Collaborate with business stakeholders to identify opportunities for system enhancements and optimizations.
- Analyze business requirements and translate them into technical solutions.
- Create, analyze, review, and modify programs for data processing and applications using JHA Symitar PowerOn programming language.
- Utilizes sound software development life cycle (SDLC) practices and accurately follows established procedures for delivering application solutions that support critical business strategies.
- Collaborates with third-party vendors to ensure the seamless implementation, maintenance, and support of software and hardware that integrates with Symitar and other applications.
- Commits application configuration updates to stage / test environment and plans for rollbacks.
- Troubleshoots issues and resolving incidents in support of existing production applications and initiates action to correct quality problems or notifies others of issues as appropriate.

Reports to: Application Development Manager

Manages: This role does not have supervisory responsibility.

### Required Education and Experience:

- Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Computer Engineering, Information Systems or other related field or equivalent work experience.
- Proficiency in one or more programming languages supporting enterprise financial institution platforms.
- Experience in a financial institution developing and supporting business applications environments.
- Experience in systems analysis, application development, testing, technical documentation, job scheduling, and report writing.
- Fundamental understanding of and experience with at least one of the following areas: Retail Operations, Lending Operations, Financial Backoffice Operations.

### Preferred Skills, Education, and Experience:

- One (1) year experience with financial institution technologies: FIS, Fiserv, FIS – Corelation KeyStone, Finastra, Jack Henry (Symitar, Synapsys, Synergy, SymXchange, ARCU), Alkami, COTS, Image Center, OpCon, etc.
- Technical hands-on experience with Jack Henry Symitar proprietary programming (PowerOn/RepGen), SQL development for relational databases, HTML, and Java Script.
- Ability to program in at least one programming language, such as C#, Java, PHP, COBOL, or RPG, HTML, JavaScript etc.
- Command of one or more scripting languages (PowerShell, Bash).
- Familiarity with the Unix operating system, Unix shell scripting, or similar.
- Project management experience in an iterative project management methodology. Ie. Agile, Scum, Kanban.
- Information Technology Infrastructure Library (ITIL) certification, or previous work within an ITIL environment.
