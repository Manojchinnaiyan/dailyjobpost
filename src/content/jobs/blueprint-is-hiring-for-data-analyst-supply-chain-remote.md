### In This Role

In this role, you will support supply chain and operations teams by ensuring critical data is accurate, complete, and available ahead of project launches. You will work hands‑on with complex, often messy datasets to build and maintain Bills of Materials (BOMs), support operational reporting, and develop insights through dashboards and analytics. This role blends technical data work with cross‑functional collaboration, helping stakeholders make informed, data‑driven decisions.

### Key Responsibilities

- Support supply chain operations by collecting, validating, and maintaining critical datasets required for upcoming initiatives and launches
- Build, maintain, and improve Bills of Materials (BOMs) to ensure supply chain data accuracy
- Develop and maintain Power BI dashboards to provide operational and supply chain insights
- Analyze inconsistent or incomplete datasets and implement improvements to data quality and reliability
- Coordinate changes to databases, including testing and implementation of updates
- Design, implement, and maintain database structures and ETL processes to support large‑scale data needs
- Write logical and physical database descriptions and define identifiers for database management systems
- Modify and optimize existing databases and reporting processes as business needs evolve
- Partner with technical, business, and operations stakeholders to define requirements, metrics, and reporting standards
- Test data systems and reports, troubleshoot issues, and implement corrections as needed

### Required Qualifications

- Bachelor’s degree in a technical or quantitative field such as Computer Science, Data Analytics, Engineering, or a related discipline
- 0–2 years of applicable professional experience in data analysis, analytics, or database support
- Strong experience using Power BI, including building dashboards and working with DAX
- Working knowledge of SQL for querying and analyzing data
- Advanced proficiency in Microsoft Excel, including pivot tables, lookup functions, and data manipulation
- Demonstrated ability to work with messy, inconsistent, or incomplete datasets and improve data accuracy
- Understanding of database technologies and ETL concepts
- Strong analytical, troubleshooting, and problem‑solving skills
- Ability to communicate data insights effectively to non‑technical stakeholders

### Preferred Qualifications

- Experience supporting data analysis in an operations or supply chain environment
- Experience building or maintaining Bills of Materials (BOMs) or similar structured datasets
- Familiarity with advanced Excel techniques such as VBA, macros, or automation
- Experience developing business analytics solutions using a combination of SQL, Excel, DAX, and Power BI
- Exposure to Azure DevOps or similar tools for work tracking and collaboration
- Strong stakeholder engagement skills, including gathering requirements and aligning on metrics and reporting goals
