### Job Overview

We are seeking a motivated and experienced Data Entry Clerk to accurately capture, maintain, and manage data across our systems. This fully remote role is well suited to a meticulous individual who works quickly and takes pride in delivering error-free results.

### Key Responsibilities

- Accurately capture and input data into company systems, spreadsheets, and databases
- Review and verify data for completeness, accuracy, and consistency
- Identify and correct data discrepancies or errors in a timely manner
- Maintain organised digital records and filing systems
- Generate basic reports and summaries from captured data as required
- Liaise with team members to clarify data requirements or resolve queries
- Handle confidential information with professionalism and discretion

### Qualifications and Experience

- 1+ years of experience in a data entry or administrative support role
- Fast and accurate typing skills (minimum 45 WPM preferred)
- Proficiency in Microsoft Excel and Google Sheets
- Strong attention to detail and commitment to data accuracy
- Ability to work independently and meet deadlines in a remote environment
- Familiarity with CRM or database management systems is advantageous
- Good written communication skills for internal correspondence
- Matric certificate required; relevant diploma or certificate is advantageous
