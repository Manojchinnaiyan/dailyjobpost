### Job Summary

Manny Construction is seeking a professional and detail-oriented Data Entry Specialist to support daily administrative and operational activities. The successful candidate will be responsible for accurately entering, updating, and maintaining company records, project documentation, invoices, and client information while ensuring efficiency and confidentiality.

### Key Responsibilities

- Accurately enter and update data into company databases and management systems
- Maintain construction project records, invoices, purchase orders, and employee timesheets
- Review data for accuracy and correct any inconsistencies
- Organize and maintain digital and physical filing systems
- Prepare reports, spreadsheets, and administrative documents for management
- Assist with project coordination and document tracking
- Communicate professionally with office staff, vendors, and subcontractors
- Ensure compliance with company procedures and recordkeeping standards

### Qualifications

- High school diploma or equivalent required
- Previous experience in data entry, administrative support, or office operations preferred
- Strong typing and computer skills with attention to detail
- Proficiency in Microsoft Office Suite, especially Excel and Word
- Excellent organizational and multitasking abilities
- Strong written and verbal communication skills
- Ability to maintain confidentiality and work independently
- Construction industry experience is a plus but not required

- Paid time off and holidays
- Opportunities for career advancement
- Professional training and development
