### **About Tunnl**

Tunnl is building a future where artificial intelligence enables organizations to connect meaningfully with the people who matter most. We help organizations conduct research at scale, define the right audiences, surface real-time insights, identify optimal communication channels, and measure changing attitudes over time.

Tunnl serves brands, agencies, and advocacy groups alike - organizations navigating complex communications, reputational, and regulatory landscapes. These teams need smarter, faster ways to make audience-informed decisions that stand up to scrutiny and resonate across stakeholder groups. Whether you're building a brand, shaping public opinion, managing risk, or launching a new initiative, Tunnl empowers you to move from insight to impact with clarity & confidence.

### The Role: Research Analyst

Tunnl is hiring a cohort of Research Analysts (0–2 years of experience) to join our team and support client work across research, data, and campaign strategy.

This role is designed for recent graduates or early-career professionals who are excited to learn how data and research inform real-world decisions across:

- Brand and market research
- Marketing and media campaigns
- Survey design and data operations

As a Research Analyst, you will work closely with our Account Management and Strategic Research teams to support projects from kickoff through delivery. You’ll gain exposure to the full research lifecycle, from survey design and data validation to reporting and client insights.

The ideal candidate is a motivated self-starter who enjoys solving problems, works well in fast-paced environments, takes pride in getting the details right, and is eager to build technical and analytical skills in survey research and microtargeting. Candidates must be located in the DC-area.

### Responsibilities

Collaborate Across Teams

- Work with Account Management, Strategic Research, and Product teams
- Join internal and client meetings, take notes, and support follow-ups
- Learn how different teams contribute to delivering client value

Work with Data & Research Tools

- Help validate datasets and review outputs for accuracy
- Assist with data formatting, tagging, and basic processing
- Learn how survey data is collected, structured, and analyzed

Build Skills & Improve Processes

- Follow internal workflows and quality control processes
- Document issues and help improve team processes over time
- Continuously develop technical, analytical, and communication skills

Support Research & Client Deliverables

- Assist in preparing client-facing materials such as data summaries (toplines, crosstabs), slide decks, and memos
- Ensure outputs are accurate, well-formatted, and easy to understand
- Support teams in turning data into clear, actionable insights

Support Survey Execution

- Assist with survey programming and testing (e.g., logic, skips, quotas)
- Monitor fieldwork and flag unusual patterns or issues
- Conduct quality checks on surveys and data outputs

### Required Qualifications

- 0–2 years of experience in survey research, market research, marketing or communications campaigns, analytics, or equivalent (internships or academic experience considered)
- Strong attention to detail and communication skills with demonstrated ability to catch and resolve errors

### Preferred Qualifications

- Exposure to different programming languages, like SQL, R, Python, etc.
- Strong written communication skills for summaries and client-facing outputs
