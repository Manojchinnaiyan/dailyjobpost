### JOB SUMMARY

Livingston International is offering a unique, hands-on internship experience for an ambitious, self-starter with experience and interest in Information Technology, Business or Supply Chain. The primary responsibility of the intern is to learn about business technology within our Global Trade Management division- processes, ins and outs and process flows. In this role, the intern will be assigned tasks that may be clerical in nature. The ideal candidate takes initiative, feels comfortable in a fast paced work environment, is highly detail-oriented, is a great communicator both written and verbal, is comfortable using technology (especially excel) to assist in the various initiatives.

### KEY DUTIES & RESPONSIBILITIES

- Heavy Data entry
- Learn key business processes as deemed necessary according to team assignment.
- Learn to use multiple systems, including proprietary software.
- Assist with tasks, projects, and/or processes.
- Complete assigned tasks on time
- Perform other related duties as assigned by management.
- Adhere to established policies and procedures.

### KNOWLEDGE & SKILLS

- Basic working knowledge of Microsoft Office products (Excel, Access, etc.)
- Strong communication skills, both verbal and written
- Professional demeanor
- Fast learner

### WORK EXPERIENCE – MINIMUM REQUIRED

- Related experience through internship or coursework
- 0-2 years of experience

### EDUCATION

- Required: Associates Degree or post-secondary education

### COMPETENCIES

- Accountability
- Agility
- Business Acumen and Straight Talk
- Customer First Focus
- Inclusion and Collaboration
- Leading and Developing
