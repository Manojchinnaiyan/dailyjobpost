We are seeking a talented Data Analyst / BI Developer to join our dynamic team. This role focuses on developing and enhancing Power BI reporting solutions to improve decision-making and execution across various business processes.

### Responsibilities

- Develop and maintain Power BI dashboards and reports to support a wide range of business processes.
- Standardize KPI and metric definitions in collaboration with stakeholders, ensuring consistent implementation across reporting assets.
- Create and optimize core semantic data models to serve multiple stakeholders and business domains.
- Build and enhance DAX measures and Power Query transformations to improve report performance and maintainability.
- Monitor and troubleshoot workspace/app refresh activities, ensuring reliable production support.
- Collaborate with team members on technical tasks and engage with subject matter experts to validate metrics and resolve data issues.
- Participate in collaborative development practices, including version control and peer reviews.

### Skills

- Bachelor's degree with at least 2 years of relevant experience, or a Master's degree with entry-level experience.
- Proven experience in data modeling and Power BI development.
- Strong analytical skills with a detail-oriented mindset.
- Excellent collaboration and communication skills, capable of managing tasks in a team environment.

### Preferred Skills

- Experience in a medical device or regulated environment.
- Familiarity with SQL, Snowflake, and Git for version control.
- Understanding of star schema and dimensional modeling in production environments.
- Knowledge of Microsoft Power Platform tools like Power Automate and Power Apps.
