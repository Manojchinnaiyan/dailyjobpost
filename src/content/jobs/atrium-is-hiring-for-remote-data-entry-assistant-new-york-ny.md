### 

Client Overview

Our client is an established retailer specializing in affordable clothing, accessories, and footwear for men and women. With a focus on providing stylish yet budget-friendly fashion, the company is seeking a detail-oriented Remote Data Entry Assistant to support their growing Merchandising and Buying teams with essential administrative and reporting functions.

Salary/Hourly Rate

$18/hr

Position Overview

The Remote Data Entry Assistant will be responsible for supporting daily operational tasks related to the purchase order life cycle. This includes creating and tracking purchase orders, assisting with sales reporting, and maintaining clear communication with internal teams and external vendors. The ideal candidate is highly organized, proficient in Excel, and able to manage multiple priorities in a fast-paced retail environment.

### Responsibilities Of The Remote Data Entry Assistant

- Enter and update purchase orders in company systems.
- Prepare weekly and monthly sales reports and data analysis.
- Communicate regularly with vendors to track order and shipment status.
- Monitor deliveries and transfers to ensure timely merchandise arrival.
- Support the buying and merchandising teams with data accuracy and order management.
- Maintain organized documentation for reporting and tracking purposes.

### Required Experience/Skills for the Remote Data Entry Assistant:

- Proficient in Microsoft Office Suite, particularly Excel and Outlook.
- Previous experience with data entry or administrative support.
- Strong written and verbal communication skills.
- Excellent attention to detail and organizational skills.
- Ability to prioritize multiple tasks and meet deadlines.
- Professional demeanor and customer-oriented attitude.

### Preferred Experience/Skills for the Remote Data Entry Assistant:

- Experience in retail, merchandising, or buying support.
- Familiarity with purchase order management systems or ERP platforms.

### Education Requirements:

High school diploma is required.
