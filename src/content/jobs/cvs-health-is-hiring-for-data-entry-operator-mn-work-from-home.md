### Position Summary

The Data Entry Operator is responsible for accurate data entry, including entering shell claims into the DG system.

### Required Qualifications

- 2+ years of data entry experience or related experience
- Able to type 60 WPM
- Proficient in all Microsoft applications
- Must be familiar with Ten Key for efficient and productive computer usage for data entry
- Ability to successfully multitask
- Must have reliable power and internet service
- Must be able to work a consistent schedule and preform daily duties reliability

### Preferred Qualifications

- Familiarity with DG
- Familiarity with Adobe Pro
- Experience with Healthcare claims
- Must be familiar with HCFA 1500 and UB Claim Forms, as well as RX, Wellness and Superbill filings

### Education

High school diploma or equivalent.
