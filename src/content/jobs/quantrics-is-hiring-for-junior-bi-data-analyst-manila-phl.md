### SPECIFIC DUTIES AND RESPONSIBILITIES:

- Ad-hoc & Executive Reporting: Service client requests for reporting across marketing and sales programs, including KPI, executive metrics, and CRTC-mandated metrics.
- Dashboard Development: Develop and maintain self-service dashboards focused on telecommunication metrics (e.g., ARPU, churn, subscriber growth, and network usage).
- Customer & Revenue Analytics: Analyze customer behavior, segmentation, and lifecycle trends including acquisition, retention, and upsell opportunities.
- Business Support and Decision Enablement: Translate complex business questions from leadership, product, and marketing teams into clear, data-driven insights.
- Data Engineering & Mapping: Gather, ingest, and map data from various sources to expand insight opportunities.
- Innovation: Contribute to the development of automation and AI technologies for enhanced reporting capabilities.

### COMPETENCIES

- 1-2 years of SQL programming (Required for data extraction and manipulation [SAS, SQL, Oracle, Teradata]).
- 1-2 years of experience with Data Visualization & Storytelling (e.g., Power BI, Tableau, MicroStrategy).
- 1-2 years of experience in Data Modeling and analysis.
- 1-2 years of experience in Excel (Pivot tables, Power Query, formulas)

Communication: Excellent English communication and presentation skills; able to explain technical findings to non-technical stakeholders.

### Complementary Competencies (Nice-to-have)

- Advanced Platform Familiarity: Previous experience or familiarity with the SAS platform, Teradata, Terra Data, or Power BI.
- Data Engineering Aptitude: Interest or experience in gathering, ingesting, and mapping data to expand reporting insights.
- Innovation Mindset: Exposure to or interest in automation and AI technologies being developed for next-generation reporting capabilities.
- Analytical Rigor: A strong mathematical or qualitative mindset coupled with analytical thinking.
- Industry Knowledge: Business acumen within the Telecommunications sector is a significant plus.
