Sarvam is building the bedrock of Sovereign AI for India. The company is developing India’s full-stack sovereign AI platform, building across research, models, infrastructure and applications with a singular focus on making AI genuinely work for India. Sarvam works with leading enterprises and public institutions and is backed by Lightspeed, Peak XV, and Khosla Ventures. Sarvam partners with India’s leading brands, including Tata Capital, SBI Life, CRED, IDFC, and LIC.

### **About the Role**

We’re looking for a Backend Intern to join Sarvam’s engineering team and work on real, production-scale systems. You’ll contribute to backend services, APIs, and data pipelines that power AI products used at population scale across India. Strong performers will be fast-tracked to a full-time offer at the end of the internship. Preferred background: AI/ML or Computer Science.

### What You’ll Do

- Build and maintain backend services and REST APIs in Python or Node.js
- Design and query SQL and NoSQL databases for AI-driven applications
- Integrate LLM APIs (such as OpenAI, Anthropic, or Sarvam’s own models) into product features
- Build and maintain data pipelines to support model training and inference workflows
- Deploy and manage services on cloud infrastructure (AWS or GCP)
- Collaborate closely with engineers and researchers to ship features end-to-end

### What We’re Looking For

- Proficiency in Python or Node.js; comfortable writing clean, maintainable backend code
- Working knowledge of REST API design and implementation
- Familiarity with SQL and at least one NoSQL database (e.g. MongoDB, Redis, or DynamoDB)
- Basic exposure to cloud platforms: AWS or GCP
- Interest in LLMs and experience or willingness to work with LLM APIs
- Background in Computer Science, AI, or Machine Learning preferred

### Bonus Points

- Prior internship or project experience with data pipelines or ML infrastructure
- Contributions to open-source projects
- Hands-on experience with vector databases or embedding workflows
- Familiarity with containerisation (Docker, Kubernetes)

- Work alongside researchers, engineers, builders, and business leaders who move fast and hold each other to a very high bar
- High ownership and high impact, from day one
