### JOB RESPONSIBILITIES:

- Implement responsive front-end styling to accurately reflect design standards across platforms.
- Own development, maintenance and support of React components.
- Add custom branding for white-label clients including logins, end-user portals, store and occasional micro site styling.
- Deliver code on time.

This position offers a hybrid work option. Nelnet values flexibility and understands the importance of work-life integration. Our hybrid work environment allows associates living within 30 miles of an office location to work remotely for part of the week, while also fostering collaboration and team connection through in-office presence three days per week.

Please note that we are unable to provide visa sponsorship for this position. To be considered, candidates must already be authorized to work in the United States without the need for current or future sponsorship.

### EDUCATION:

Bachelor’s degree in related field or equivalent experience.

### EXPERIENCE:

- Minimum 2 years total experience delivering front end code in a corporate environment.
- Depth of experience with React, and solid experience with ES6/7 JavaScript.
- Experience using Design & Prototyping Programs (preferably knowledge of Figma and Illustrator), including slicing from layered design files.
- Experience in other programming languages (such as C#, Java, PHP, ASP, ColdFusion, etc.).
- Experience with React, HTML5, CSS3, SASS, and cross-browser development.-Required
- Comfort working on AI-related initiatives, proofs of concept, and product enhancements as part of a broader engineering role.
