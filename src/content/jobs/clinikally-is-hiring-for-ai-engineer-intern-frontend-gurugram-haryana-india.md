****About the job:****

At Clinikally, we’re building the future of personalized dermatology and haircare in India through AI-powered experiences and expert-led care. Backed by leading global investors and trusted by over 3 million users in just 3.5 years, we’re scaling rapidly and looking for ambitious engineers who want to build real-world AI products used at scale.

### Role Overview

As an AI Engineering Intern, you’ll work on Clara, Clinikally’s multi-agent AI platform for dermatology and haircare. This role is heavily focused on frontend integration and production-ready AI experiences — building interfaces, workflows, and user-facing AI systems rather than experimental demos.

You’ll collaborate closely with engineering, product, and AI teams to ship scalable AI features directly into production.

### What You’ll Work On

- Build and integrate AI-powered user experiences using React / Next.js
- Develop production-grade interfaces for AI workflows, agent interactions, and recommendation systems
- Work on agent orchestration flows, tool calling, and frontend interaction layers for Clara’s multi-agent architecture
- Build dynamic UI components for chat flows, AI-generated insights, image analysis, and product recommendations
- Integrate APIs, streaming responses, and structured AI outputs into responsive frontend applications
- Contribute to multimodal experiences including image uploads, OCR flows, and vision-assisted diagnosis journeys
- Improve performance, usability, and reliability of AI-powered workflows in production
- Collaborate with backend and AI teams on prompt flows, evaluations, and product experimentation

### Must-Have Skills

- Strong proficiency in React.js / Next.js and modern frontend development
- Good understanding of JavaScript/TypeScript, component architecture, and API integrations
- Familiarity with Python and working with LLM APIs (OpenAI, Anthropic, Gemini, etc.)
- Understanding of AI product workflows, structured outputs, and conversational interfaces
- Experience with Git and collaborative development workflows

### Strong Plus

- Experience integrating AI/LLM features into frontend applications
- Familiarity with streaming responses, chat UIs, and real-time frontend experiences
- Knowledge of multi-agent systems, tool calling, or orchestration frameworks
- Understanding of prompt engineering and AI evaluation workflows
- Experience working with vector databases or RAG-based systems

### Nice to Have

- FastAPI, Docker, or Redis
- Basic understanding of backend architecture and deployment workflows
- Experience with image processing or multimodal AI applications

### What We’re Looking For

- Engineers who enjoy building polished, production-ready products
- Strong ownership mindset with attention to performance and user experience
- Curiosity about AI systems and how they translate into real customer-facing applications
- Ability to move fast, learn quickly, and collaborate across teams
