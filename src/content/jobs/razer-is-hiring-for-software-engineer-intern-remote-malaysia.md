Joining Razer means being part of a global mission to revolutionize the way the world games. - Experiment with AI solutions such as TTS (text-to-speech) and STT (speech-to-text).
- Collaborate with mentors and teammates to design, implement, and deliver software that scales.

- Best practices in backend development with Python and modern frameworks.
- Building robust CI/CD pipelines for continuous integration and deployment.
- How to integrate AI/ML services into real applications.
- Cloud technologies (AWS) and containerization (Docker, Kubernetes).
- Collaborating in a professional engineering team with code reviews, sprints, and agile workflows.
