## Responsibilities

- Maintain Figma component library and contribution guidelines.

- Review PRs for design fidelity.

- Figma auto-layout and variables expertise.
- Portfolio showing systems thinking, not just polished screens.
- WCAG 2.1 AA — you can audit and fix accessibility issues, not just flag them.
