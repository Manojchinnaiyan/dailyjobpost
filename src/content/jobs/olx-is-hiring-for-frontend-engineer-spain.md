### Join Our Team as a Frontend Engineer!

Join one of our cross-functional teams in Motors, Real Estate, OLX Classifieds or Platforms, working remotely from Spain. As part of our 1,000+ tech team across multiple locations, you’ll collaborate with engineers, SREs, and product teams to build scalable, high-impact solutions, while reporting to an Engineering Manager who will support your growth and impact.

You'll work on a mixed tech stack based on a microservices architecture, entirely on Amazon Web Services, with open source libs like React and/or NextJS.

If you’re craving the excitement of tackling scalable problems and optimizing solutions, we’d love to hear from you!

### Who we are looking for:

- Someone eager to understand how things work and embrace a "you build it, you run it, you own it" mindset.
- Passionate about architecting, building, and scaling cloud-based systems for millions of users.
- Proficient in TypeScript & Node.js.
- Skilled in writing clean, maintainable, and well-documented code with a strong understanding of testing.
- A problem solver who approaches challenges with both a technical and user-first mindset to build impactful solutions.
- A team player who is open to discussions, cross-team collaboration, and knowledge sharing.

### What you will be doing:

- Contributing technically to projects and taking part in the planning, execution, and success of complex technical projects.
- Establishing credibility with the quality of your own and your team’s technical execution.
- Encouraging the team to innovate, invent, implement and deploy complex software solutions.
- Asking questions, brainstorming and owning the product we are building.
- Working closely with the product management team to ensure we’re building the best products for our users.
